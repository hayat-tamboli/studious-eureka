## Context

IIT Bombay operates an electric-vehicle fleet that shuttles between important points on campus. The project focused on improving the fleet’s efficiency and user experience through a new app, a refreshed identity for the service, and route-optimization ideas.

## Understanding the existing system

### Stakeholders

- Students and other passengers
- EV drivers
- Fleet or campus-transport operators

### User survey

> Describe how the survey was conducted and what you wanted to understand about the EV system and stakeholder interactions.

### What we learned

- Students often prefer autos because the EVs have limited availability.
- The fleet consists of 15 EVs managed by 9 drivers.
- Charging takes approximately 3–4 hours, depending on station capacity.
- The service operates approximately from 6 AM to 10 PM, with EVs arriving at stops every 5 minutes.

## Defining the opportunity

### Problem statement

> How might we improve the efficiency and availability of IIT Bombay’s EV shuttle system while creating a clearer experience for passengers and operators?

### Design goals

- Improve the discoverability and availability of EV rides.
- Make routes and waiting times easier to understand.
- Support more efficient fleet and charging operations.
- Create a recognizable and approachable identity for the service.
