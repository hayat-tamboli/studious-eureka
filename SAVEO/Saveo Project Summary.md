# Saveo

## tl;dr

**What**
College students struggle with finances and lack structure to overcome their money-related issues.

**Why**
Personal finance management-related issues should not be a part of the college experience.

**My role**
End-to-end design solution, from research and the pitch to design and detailed prototyping.

**Result**
A conceptual mobile application to help busy college students with their personal finance management and goals.

## Context
This was a college project where we were provided with a challenge of designing an application which would help students understand and manage their finances over their student journey, we were given 2 weeks to design and prototype a possible solution and were asked to work in a group of 3 people.

## Unpacking student finances
We studied college students’ spending habits, saving preferences, and financial challenges and performed **one on one interviews** . We also reviewed existing finance apps to understand how they support students and where opportunities for improvement exist.

## Selected idea but in depth
1. We use account aggregators to get users' data and use it to show their savings, current goals
2. We allow users to create multiple rules regarding their goals and let them generate automations for saving money.
3. We would list products with special student discounts and which would potentially help students.

## Testing the concept
![[image 108.png|324]]![[image 109.png|333]]

![[WhatsApp Image 2023-08-13 at 20.55 1.png|661]]

We conducted user testing with 12 users of varied backgrounds.
Many people felt confused with the nature of rules but appreciated the overall idea of integrating saving habits with day to day life activities.
Some constructive feedback we decoded was
1. We should be allowing users to view their budget data in multiple time frames (Daily, weekly, monthly)
2. Better expenditure analytics should be added to let them get aware about their spending habits
3. More rule types, connected to other data which can be gathered from users' daily life, such as fitness data, digital wellbeing data, etc
4. Allowing users to withdraw money from one goal to re-allocate it another.
5. Addition of more categories of products in the Explore section.
## Reflection
This design project work was a part of a 2 week module at IDC, IIT Bombay, where we were provided with constant constructive feedback from professors and peers which allowed us to conceptualise faster and better
Our focus was on creating a platform that seamlessly integrates students' savings goals with their regular habits and lifestyle and provide them with an intuitive interface to help them save more.
### Think in systems.
It's not just about designing an intuitive application, but also how it fits into the existing system which users' have and integrating the feature within it to resolve their issues seamlessly and also motivate them to improve their lifestyle objectively.

### Lack of nudging
A wonderful feature sitting in side an app wouldn't make the users use it more, we should be adding more nudges in order to push users in using our feature more, making more rules, and hence saving more.
